CRM Executive Daily Routine

MORNING (9:00 - 10:00 AM)
  • Check overnight leads - Review all NEW leads
  • Verify data completeness
  • Confirm WATI welcome sent
  • Start Attempt 1 calls
CALLING BLOCK 1 (10:00 AM - 1:00 PM)
  • Execute 5-day attempt calls
  • Log every call in CRM
  • Mark outcomes correctly
  • Warm lead follow-ups
CALLING BLOCK 2 (2:00 - 5:00 PM)
  • Qualification calls - Score all 8 criteria
  • Engaged lead callbacks - Priority
  • Must call within specified time
END OF DAY (5:00 - 6:00 PM)
  • All calls logged
  • All outcomes updated
  • Notes added
  • Daily report submission

BDM Daily Routine

MORNING (9:00 - 10:00 AM)
  • Review hot leads assigned
  • Check pending consultations
  • Review today's site visits
  • Prepare materials
FIELD WORK (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM)
  • Conduct consultations (Phone/Video/In-person)
  • Execute site visits
  • Capture photos & assessment data
  • Update CRM immediately
OFFICE WORK (4:00 - 6:00 PM)
  • Create proposals for visited sites
  • Send via WhatsApp + Email
  • Proposal follow-up calls
  • Upload all photos to CRM

Team Leader Daily Routine

MORNING (9:00 - 10:00 AM)
  • Review yesterday's performance
  • Check pending approvals dashboard
  • Process 5-day reviews, Junk requests, Lost requests
THROUGHOUT DAY
  • Quality monitoring - Random call listening
  • CRM data audit
  • Support CRM executives with difficult leads
  • Handle customer escalations
END OF DAY (5:00 - 6:00 PM)
  • Team performance review
  • Clear all pending approvals
  • Brief Sales Manager on key issues
