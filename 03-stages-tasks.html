Post-Qualification Task Distribution

🔥 HOT LEAD - 8 Tasks Over 60 Days

Objective: Push towards consultation, site visit, proposal, closure

Week 1 - Task 1 (Day 1-2)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Book consultation call/visit
Call Script: "Consultation slot confirm பண்ணலாமா? இந்த week available-ஆ?"
✓ Success: Consultation booked → Create Site Visit task

Week 2 - Task 2 (Day 7-8)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Follow up on consultation/site visit
  • • If done: Proceed to proposal
  • • If not done: Reschedule, understand blocker

Week 3 - Task 3 (Day 14-15)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Proposal follow-up OR second consultation push
Share: ROI calculator, EMI options

Week 4 - Task 4 (Day 21-22)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Address objections from proposal
Share: Customer testimonial, subsidy info

Week 5 - Task 5 (Day 28-29)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Push for commitment
Offer: Limited time offer if available

Week 6 - Task 6 (Day 35-36)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Decision maker connect
If family involved: Offer family consultation

Week 7 - Task 7 (Day 42-43)

Owner:
BDM
Action:
Final negotiation
Escalate: Involve Sales Manager if needed

Week 8 - Task 8 (Day 56-60)

Owner:
BDM + Sales Manager
Action:
Close or extend
If not closed: TL review → Extend 30 days OR move to Warm

🌤️ WARM LEAD - 4 Tasks Over 30 Days

Objective: Build interest, educate, move to Hot

Week 1 - Task 1 (Day 3-4)

Owner:
CRM Executive
Action:
Share brochure, video, answer questions
Goal:
Gauge real interest level

Week 2 - Task 2 (Day 10-11)

Owner:
CRM Executive
Action:
Share customer story, ROI example
Goal:
Build trust and urgency

Week 3 - Task 3 (Day 17-18)

Owner:
CRM Executive
Action:
Offer consultation/site assessment
Goal:
Convert to Hot

Week 4 - Task 4 (Day 25-28)

Owner:
CRM Executive
Action:
Final check, book consultation or extend nurturing
  • • If interested: Move to HOT, create 8 tasks
  • • If not ready: Move to COLD nurturing

❄️ COLD LEAD - 16 Week Nurturing

Objective: Stay in touch, re-engage when ready
Frequency: One WhatsApp message every 2 weeks
Owner: Automation (no manual tasks unless customer responds)
Week 2 Market update message
Week 4 Festival/seasonal offer
Week 6 Customer success story
Week 8 EMI/financing highlight
Week 10 Subsidy update message
Week 12 New offer announcement
Week 14 Re-engagement check ("Still interested?")
Week 16 Final message before archive
If customer responds at any point:
→ Move to WARM
→ Create 4 follow-up tasks
→ BDM calls within 4 hours
After 16 weeks with no response:
→ Move to Archive
→ Yearly touchpoint only (Pongal, Diwali)

Key Stage Definitions

NEW

Entry: Lead enters CRM from any source
Max time: 4 hours
Auto-actions:
  • Welcome WhatsApp within 5 minutes
  • Create Attempt 1 task
  • Assign to CRM Executive

5-DAY ATTEMPT IN PROGRESS

Owner: CRM Executive
Actions: 5 call attempts with WhatsApp fallback
Exit: Connected OR All 5 attempts done
Max time: 6 days (including TL review)

CONTACTED

Entry: Customer connected on call OR replied to WhatsApp
Owner: CRM Executive
Actions: Complete qualification call (Score all 8 criteria)
Max time: 24 hours from contact

QUALIFIED (Hot/Warm/Cold)

Entry: Scoring complete
Sub-stages: HOT / WARM / COLD
Owner: BDM (Hot), CRM Executive (Warm/Cold)
Actions: Begin appropriate task cycle

CONSULTATION SCHEDULED

Owner: BDM
Max time: 7 days (must reschedule if delayed)
Auto-actions:
  • Calendar event created
  • Reminder 24 hours before
  • Reminder 2 hours before
