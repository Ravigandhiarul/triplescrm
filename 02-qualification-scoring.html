When to Qualify

Qualification happens ONLY when:

  • Customer connected on call during 5-day attempt, OR
  • Customer replied to WhatsApp (engaged), OR
  • Customer called the company number

Qualification Call Opening

🎯 First 30 Seconds (Tanglish)

"வணக்கம் {Name}! Triples Energy-ல இருந்து {CRMName} பேசுறேன்.

8 Qualification Questions

Q1

Monthly EB Bill

"மாசம் approximately எவ்வளவு EB bill வரும்?"
> ₹10,000 15 points
₹5,000 - ₹10,000 12 points
₹3,000 - ₹5,000 8 points
< ₹3,000 4 points
Don't know 5 points
Q2

Property Ownership

"இது own house-ஆ, rent-ஆ?"
Own property 15 points
Rented (owner consent) 8 points
Not specified 5 points
Rented (no consent) 0 points
Q3

Roof Type & Space

"Roof எந்த type? RCC-ஆ, sheet-ஆ, tiles-ஆ?"
"Approximately எவ்வளவு space இருக்கும்?"
RCC with 300+ sqft 10 points
RCC with 200-300 sqft 8 points
Sheet roof suitable 6 points
Limited space 3 points
Not suitable 0 points
Q4

Timeline / Urgency

"எப்போ install பண்ண plan?"
Within 1 month 15 points
1-3 months 12 points
3-6 months 8 points
6-12 months 4 points
Just exploring 2 points
Q5

Decision Maker

"நீங்களே decide பண்ணுவீங்களா, family-கிட்ட discuss பண்ணணுமா?"
Self decision 10 points
With spouse 8 points
Family/parents 5 points
Multiple stakeholders 3 points
Q6

Budget / Financing

"EMI option பாக்குறீங்களா, full payment-ஆ?"
"Approximately budget என்ன range?"
Full payment ready 10 points
EMI interested 8 points
Loan needed 6 points
Budget constraint 3 points
Not discussed 5 points
Q7

Awareness / Interest Level

"Solar பத்தி முன்னாடி யாராவது explain பண்ணாங்களா?"
"Tata Power Solar பத்தி தெரியுமா?"
Knows well, ready 10 points
Some awareness 7 points
First time exploring 5 points
Skeptical 3 points
Q8

Engagement During Call

(CRM Executive observation)
Very engaged, asking questions 15 points
Responsive, positive tone 12 points
Neutral, giving short answers 8 points
Distracted, rushing 4 points
Reluctant, negative 2 points

Lead Temperature Classification

Maximum Score: 100 points

🔥 HOT LEAD

70-100 points

Auto-Actions:

  • Tag: HOT
  • Priority: High
  • Book consultation within 24 hours
  • Create 8 follow-up tasks (60 days)
  • Assign to senior BDM
  • Send Hot Lead Welcome WhatsApp

🌤️ WARM LEAD

50-69 points

Auto-Actions:

  • Tag: WARM
  • Priority: Medium
  • Start nurturing sequence
  • Create 4 follow-up tasks (30 days)
  • Send Warm Lead Welcome + brochure

❄️ COLD LEAD

30-49 points

Auto-Actions:

  • Tag: COLD
  • Priority: Low
  • Move to 16-week nurturing
  • Monthly touchpoint only
  • Send Cold Lead Acknowledgment

🚫 JUNK LEAD

Below 30 points

Requires TL Approval:

  • Tag: JUNK (Pending TL Approval)
  • Create Task: "TL Review - Junk"
  • TL must approve with reason
  • If approved: Send closure message
