🔥 5-Day Initial Contact Attempt System NEW

Objective: Ensure every new lead receives 5 structured contact attempts within 5 days before being marked as unresponsive. No lead is closed without completing this cycle + TL approval.

Day 0 - Lead Entry

  • Lead enters CRM
  • Auto-assign to CRM Executive based on area
  • Create 5 Tasks: Attempt 1 through Attempt 5
  • Trigger Welcome WhatsApp (Message A1)
  • Task 1 due: Within 4 hours

Day 1 - Attempt 1

  • CRM Executive calls lead
  • IF CONNECTED: Proceed to Qualification → Cancel remaining tasks
  • IF NOT CONNECTED: Mark attempt, auto-send WhatsApp Day 1 Message
  • Task 2 becomes active (due: Day 2)

Day 2-4 - Attempts 2, 3, 4

  • Same flow as Day 1
  • Each unsuccessful attempt triggers WhatsApp
  • Minimum 20-hour gap between attempts
  • Vary timing (morning, afternoon, evening)

Day 5 - Attempt 5 (Final)

  • CRM Executive makes final call
  • IF CONNECTED: Proceed to Qualification
  • IF NOT CONNECTED: Send WhatsApp Day 5 Final Message
  • Create Task: "TL Review Required" (Due: Day 6)

Day 6 - Team Leader Review

  • TL reviews all 5 attempts
  • TL checks WhatsApp delivery/read status
  • TL MUST choose one option

⚠️ Team Leader Decision (Day 6)

  • OPTION A: Retry (3 more attempts over 3 days)
  • OPTION B: Move to Cold Nurturing (16-week drip)
  • OPTION C: Mark as Junk (with reason)

⚡ No lead moves without TL action

📌 Important Rules

  1. Tasks are SEQUENTIAL - cannot skip Attempt 2 to do Attempt 3
  2. Each attempt must have call log entry (time, duration, outcome)
  3. WhatsApp auto-triggers ONLY if call marked "Not Connected"
  4. If customer calls back at any point → Mark as Connected, proceed
  5. If customer replies to WhatsApp → Mark as Engaged, BDM calls within 30 mins
  6. Minimum gap between attempts: 20 hours
  7. Call attempts should vary timing (morning, afternoon, evening)
