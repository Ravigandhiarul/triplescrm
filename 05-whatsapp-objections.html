📌 Objective

After site visit/proposal, customers often stall due to specific objections. This section provides objection-specific nurturing tracks with manual tasks + automated WhatsApp over 60 days (8 weeks).

7 Objection Categories:

  • PRICE - "விலை அதிகம்"
  • EMI/FINANCING - "EMI/Loan issue"
  • ROOF SUITABILITY - "Roof problem"
  • FAMILY DECISION - "Family-கிட்ட கேக்கணும்"
  • SUBSIDY DOUBTS - "Subsidy கிடைக்குமா?"
  • TIMING/DELAY - "இப்போ வேண்டாம், later"
  • COMPETITOR - "வேற company பாக்குறேன்"
